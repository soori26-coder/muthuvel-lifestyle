Tiruppur

Designed, cut, sewn, and packed in Tiruppur.

One coordinated supply chain. One accountable production team.

Original styles are created here, then cut, sewn, and packed in a 7,000 sq.ft. facility.

The studio develops its own collections and also creates styles from a buyer brief, mood board, sketch, or reference sample. The floor then covers the cut-to-pack stages. Upstream textile work — knitting, dyeing, compacting, fabric finishing, printing, and embroidery — is done with selected processing partners in Tiruppur.

Muthuvel manages product development, sourcing coordination, production planning, garment manufacturing, quality control, finishing, packing, and buyer communication.

In-house

Cut to pack

Fabric inspection and preparation. Marker planning, spreading, and precision cutting. Sewing and assembly. In-process quality control. Thread trimming, pressing, and folding. Packing, carton preparation, and shipment coordination.

Tiruppur partners

Textile processing

Knitting yarn into the required fabric. Dyeing and wet processing to the buyer’s colour. Compacting and finishing for stability and hand feel. Printing, embroidery, and appliqué where the style needs them.
